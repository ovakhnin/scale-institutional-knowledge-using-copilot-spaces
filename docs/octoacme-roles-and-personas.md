# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsor / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors provide strategic sponsorship, executive support, and decision-making authority for the initiative. They help align the project with business goals and ensure the team has the support needed to deliver value.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm the business case and strategic value of the initiative
- Secure budget, staffing, and stakeholder sponsorship
- Approve major scope, timeline, or priority changes
- Help resolve escalations that exceed team-level authority
- Review milestone outcomes and business impact with the PM and Product Manager

### Goals
- Maximize business value and strategic alignment
- Maintain executive sponsorship and stakeholder confidence
- Enable timely decisions that reduce delivery friction

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee updates, executive reviews, and milestone check-ins
- Strategic risk and investment discussions with PM and Product lead stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Project Managers: align priorities, confirm trade-offs, and support escalations
- With Product Managers: validate business outcomes, funding, and strategic direction
- With Developers and Technical Leads: provide high-level constraints and unblock cross-functional issues when needed

---

## Product Owner

### Role Summary
A Product Owner translates business priorities into actionable backlog items and ensures the team delivers the highest-value work. They work closely with the Product Manager on prioritization while turning intent into clear, testable requirements for delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Refine backlog items and acceptance criteria with the delivery team
- Clarify requirements, user stories, and dependencies
- Prioritize day-to-day work in collaboration with the Product Manager
- Validate that delivered work meets customer needs and business intent
- Partner with QA and Developers to resolve ambiguity before release

### Goals
- Maintain a clear and prioritized backlog
- Reduce ambiguity and rework during delivery
- Ensure work reflects the most important customer and business outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Backlog refinement, sprint planning, and product review meetings
- Acceptance criteria discussions with Developers and QA

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Product Managers: align strategic direction and prioritization decisions
- With Developers: clarify work, definitions of done, and user value
- With Project Managers: provide sequencing, dependency updates, and readiness for milestones
- With QA: confirm acceptance criteria and validation expectations

---

## UX / UI Designer or Researcher

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and Researchers bring user needs, usability, and design quality into the project. They help ensure that product decisions are informed by customer behavior, accessibility, and interface consistency.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research, interviews, usability testing, and feedback synthesis
- Define user flows, interaction patterns, and interface design decisions
- Collaborate with Product and Engineering to translate requirements into usable experiences
- Assess accessibility, consistency, and usability risks
- Support design validation before release

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and valuable user experiences
- Turn user insight into actionable product decisions
- Reduce risk of usability issues late in delivery

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews, user research sessions, and validation checkpoints
- Collaboration with Product, Engineering, and QA during iteration

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Product Managers: validate concepts against customer needs and success metrics
- With Developers: align design implementation and technical constraints
- With PMs: raise design-related risks or timeline impacts early
- With QA: help define usability and acceptance checks for experience quality

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects guide solution design, technical quality, and implementation standards. They help the team make sound engineering decisions while keeping delivery aligned with architecture, platform constraints, and long-term maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction, architecture, and integration patterns
- Review design trade-offs and technical risks with the delivery team
- Mentor Developers and ensure alignment with standards and practices
- Coordinate technical dependencies across services, systems, or teams
- Support release readiness by identifying implementation or operational risks

### Goals
- Deliver maintainable, scalable, and secure technical solutions
- Reduce avoidable architecture drift and integration issues
- Support the team in making high-confidence engineering decisions

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews, technical design discussions, and implementation planning
- Cross-team dependency and risk updates with PM and Product stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Developers: guide design decisions, review solutions, and unblock technical issues
- With Product Managers: help assess feasibility, trade-offs, and delivery risk
- With Project Managers: communicate technical dependencies, sequencing, and risk impacts
- With QA: align on testability, environment needs, and validation strategy

---

## Release Manager / Operations / Site Reliability Role

### Role Summary
Release, Operations, or Site Reliability roles ensure that changes are ready to deploy, observable, and safe to operate in production. They help reduce release risk and coordinate the operational aspects of delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare deployment plans, release checklists, and rollback readiness
- Monitor production health and support early detection of issues
- Coordinate release windows, communication, and operational validation
- Partner with Developers and QA on deployment readiness and smoke tests
- Support incident response and post-incident learning when needed

### Goals
- Reduce risk during deployment and production operations
- Improve observability, recovery, and service reliability
- Ensure releases align with operational readiness standards

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews, deployment planning, incident coordination, and post-release checks
- Operational dashboards, alerts, and stakeholder notifications

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- With Project Managers: align deployment timing, stakeholder communication, and risk tracking
- With Developers: validate production readiness and support monitoring needs
- With QA: confirm test coverage, smoke checks, and release verification
- With Product Managers and Sponsors: communicate readiness, customer impact, and business constraints

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- In small teams, these responsibilities may be combined into a single person; the Project Manager remains the coordinator for cross-role dependencies and delivery alignment.
